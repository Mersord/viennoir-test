What is VIEN

A world of its own

An unexpected journey through music, stories, physical artistry and the mysterious.

Vien.noir combines the energy of a concert with the intimacy of cabaret and the visual power of grand stage design. Every evening is conceived as a complete experience – sensual, stylish and with a hint of danger.

The world of Vien.noir

Six elements. One experience.

We do not merely tell stories. We create an evening that begins the moment you enter and continues to resonate long after the final curtain.

01

Powerful voices, live arrangements and music that carries every scene.
