The story

Where desire meets Viennese society.

A Viennese scandal – Bridgerton inspired.

In a society where every word is a weapon and every touch a promise, a love triangle ignites. A lavish ball becomes the stage for intrigue, longing and choices that no one can undo.

Pop meets classical music, powerful voices meet aerial acrobatics and opulent costumes meet Viennese theatricality. Season of Desire is romantic, sensual and dramatic – with a modern pulse beneath its historical surface.
